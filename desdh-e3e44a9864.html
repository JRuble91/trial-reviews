Week 1: the damage is there, the deaths aren't fixed yet

Her damage was second among our melee on her first night, at 99% of the raid's DPS median, and that was with the second-lowest item level in the raid (329 against a raid median of 332). Melee is our thin side, and she's already on this week's board in group 1 as the C1 kicker.

What has to change is how often she dies early. She was among the first four deaths 2.8 times per 10 pulls, against 1.8 for the raid middle (20th of 22). On five pulls she died to Vexhul's melee in the first 2–7 seconds. She used a healthstone on only 2 of 29 pulls and never pressed Darkness.

This page grows each week. The tracker below gets a new row after every raid night, and the call at the end of the watch rests on the five targets.

Recommendation: Main raider, Bench, or Pass

A provisional call after one night. It firms up as the tracker fills in.

Main raider Leaning

Her damage already fits a melee spot, and she's slotted as a C1 kicker. This becomes a firm yes if her early deaths drop to the raid middle over the next two weeks.

Bench

The fallback if the deaths don't improve. The damage is good, but a melee who dies early on Twin Fangs costs us a Globule grabber, a Feast soaker and a kicker all at once.

Pass

Nothing points here yet. It would take poor attendance, or no change after the deaths are raised with her directly.

For main

What she brings

  • Damage: 99% of the DPS median on a typical pull, 2nd of 6 melee (Dishwasher 105%), at ilvl 329.
  • Soaks and Globules at the raid middle: Feast soaks 0.71 a pull (raid 0.71), and Globule grabs about mid-raid by our count.
  • Little avoidable damage: the least Deadly Venom and Coiling Ichor damage of any melee.
  • A DPS potion every pull (29 of 29).
  • Mythic experience: 4/10 with Timeless (12 kills), Heroic 10/10.
Against, for now

What's holding it back

  • Early deaths: 2.8 per 10 pulls against a 1.8 middle, 20th of 22. She was the first death on 3 pulls.
  • Dying at the pull: killed by Vexhul's melee 1.5 s into pulls 7, 11, 12 and 18, and 7 s into pull 13. On pulls 7 and 18 she was battle-rezzed, which cost the raid its rez.
  • Defensives: healthstone on 2 of 29 pulls, Blur on 7, and no Darkness all night.
  • Her Wipefest score was 21st of 22 (63 against a middle of 70).
How the call gets made

Five targets by early December

  1. Early deaths at or under the raid middle.
  2. No deaths in the first 10 seconds of a pull.
  3. Lands her C1 Visceral Burst kicks (new assignment this week).
  4. Healthstone in every pull that goes past the first Feast, and Darkness used where it's called.
  5. Damage stays at or above the DPS median.

4 or 5 met → main raider. 2 or 3 → bench. 0 or 1, or missed nights → pass.
