Where she stands: a useful flex player, not ahead of anyone yet

She has filled whatever spot was open on all four nights: ranged DPS on Sszorak (including the kill), then Gladrix's healing spot on Twin Fangs. That flexibility is the real value.

The numbers put her at the bottom of each group she's played in. As Elemental, her damage was mid-pack (about 95% of our ranged median), but she died early 1.5 to 2 times as often as the raid middle. As Restoration, she did the least healing of the four healers on both Twin Fangs nights. She also had the lowest overhealing, so she isn't wasting casts, she's just behind on output.

The fixable part is mechanics. On Sszorak she took more tornado (Tempest) debuffs than any other ranged player. On Twin Fangs she grabbed the fewest Globules in the raid. Healers also skipped potions. Keep her on the trial as a healer first. Re-check after two more Twin Fangs nights on three things: Globule grabs, a potion every pull, and Spirit Link use.

The log can't show assignments. If healers are told to skip Globules, this isn't on her. But the other healers did grab.

What to work on, and what to ask her

Work on

  • Globules: grab one every Deluge unless she's assigned off. It's the single biggest gap on our current boss.
  • A potion every pull, as a healer too. Healthstone in every pull that goes past the first Feast.
  • Spirit Link: agree with Aeryx on which Brood or Feast each one covers, so two SLTs actually get used.
  • As Elemental: step out of tornadoes, and stay out of the front of the boss (Claws).

Ask her

  • Is she done with Timeless? They raid the same nights we do. Her last Timeless log is Oct 27.
  • Which spec does she want to be judged on? Elemental is her stronger history, but the open need has been heals.
  • Is Healing Tide Totem in her build, and did anyone give her a Globule assignment on Nov 10 or 12?
  • Who presses lust when she's Elemental with a Mage in the raid?
